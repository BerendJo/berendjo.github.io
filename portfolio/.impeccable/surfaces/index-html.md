# Surface brief: portfolio home

Scope: index.html, mode Experience. Visitor is a hiring manager or artist scanning for fit. Action: see the work, then email. World is inherited from the PDF (established identity), no concept tournament. Unresolved: hero approval, YouTube link, project page layout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio PDF cover, made physical. One huge tight word, "Portfolio", owns the page; the interface recedes. Refuses the centered-hero-plus-three-cards portfolio template.
OWN-WORLD: Pale pink paper (#fff4f4) with faint grain, near-black ink, no accent. Archivo 800 at the narrowest useful width with -0.06em tracking. Halftone ASCII fragments (DJ deck, lock, cup) printed multiply onto the paper. Sharp corners, hairline ink rules.
STORY: Berend is a graphic designer from Breda with range (brand, music, events). The visitor understands that in seconds, scrolls to the work, and emails.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim nav top (name left; Werk, Over, Contact, NL/EN right). The ASCII DJ deck bleeds in from the top right, partly off canvas. Giant "Portfolio" bottom-left, "Berend Joosen" beneath it, one short line and one CTA "Bekijk werk". The grayscale cut-out portrait stands at the right, cropped by the bottom edge, overlapping the ASCII.
FORM: Poster cover, form 1 of 1 (inherited from the PDF), seed none (no roll: established world).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
