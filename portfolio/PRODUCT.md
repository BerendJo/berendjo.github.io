# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JS. No build step, no framework (same approach as the BEJO music site in `../music`). Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Hiring managers, design studios and agencies, and music artists or venues looking for a graphic designer. They arrive from a CV, an email or a link in a message, scan quickly, and decide whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Personal graphic design portfolio of Berend Joosen (24, graphic designer from Breda, Netherlands). It turns the 18-slide PDF "Berend Portfolio 2026 NL" into a website. Success: a visitor sees the range of the work, understands what Berend can be hired for, and emails berendjoosen@gmail.com.

## Positioning

Graphic designer who is also a DJ and producer (BEJO). Commissions for artists (album covers, logos) in a personal grungy style, alongside brand and web work (JDE internship, Edufort rebrand) and event identity (Krakatoa, MEZZ).

## Operating Context

Source material is the PDF in the repo root (`Berend Portfolio 2026 NL.pdf`, 16:9 slides, Dutch). Work is shown through images extracted from that PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages: Dutch and English with a toggle. The PDF is Dutch; the English copy is translated from it.
- Structure: overview of the five projects that opens a separate page per project (confirmed).
- Projects: Douwe Egberts (stage), Edufort (afstudeerstage), Graphic Design commissies, Krakatoa (techno evenement), MEZZ (opdracht).
- Skills named in the PDF per project (Figma, Adobe Suite, SEO, front-end, brand design, motion, event and social media).
- The YouTube link on the MEZZ slide in the PDF may be truncated (`youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZjFFhqk37GI&si=...`). It must be confirmed with Berend before it is shipped as a working link.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Berend Joosen. Contact: berendjoosen@gmail.com.
- The PDF's look is the established identity: pale pink ground, very tight heavy grotesque headings in black, small body text, ASCII and halftone texture fragments. Keep it.
- Relationship to the BEJO music site: related person, separate site, separate look.

## Evidence on Hand

Real project imagery inside the PDF (posters, mockups, phone screens, album covers, portrait). Not available: testimonials, client logos, metrics. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; the interface recedes.
2. Every claim comes from the PDF. No invented clients, results or numbers.
3. One clear action: get in touch.
4. Works on a phone as well as on a desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, alt text on every image, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, language attribute switches with the NL/EN toggle.
